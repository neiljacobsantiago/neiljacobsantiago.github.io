May 3, 2026 | Concert Media

Aurora Fest 2026:
Day 2

Documenting a massive music festival requires pure endurance. Day 2 of Aurora Fest was an absolute gauntlet. We arrived at CBC around 10:40 AM and queued in line by 11 AM, eventually marking a spot somewhat close to the platinum barricade in the front at 1 PM.

Festival Prep
Crowd View

Survival in the extreme heat began and lasted until dusk. Then, the OPM concert itself finally kicked off in this exact order: Kitchie Nadal, Parokya Ni Edgar, Kamikazee, Sexbomb Girls, IV Of Spades, and SB19.

The dynamic stage lighting—shifting from deep purples to blinding strobes—meant shooting purely in manual and making split-second exposure adjustments. We experienced over 12 hours of extreme standing duration, waiting for those perfect, high-energy moments from the artists.

Neil at Aurora Fest
Festival Energy

After navigating massive crowds and battling the elements, we exited the AMF vicinity at around 1:50 AM, finally arriving home completely exhausted at 4:10 AM.

"Grabe yung pagod, as in legit—you really need to be physically and mentally prepared for a setup like this. May mga flaws and issues yung event, pero for a first-timer being that close to the artists? Not bad at all. Pumunta talaga ako para sa IVOS, pero I didn't expect na sobrang mapapa-wild ako sa set ng PNE at KMKZ; the raw energy of the crowd and live OPM just pushes you through the exhaustion. After all the chaos, the subsequent Lightroom editing sessions are where the magic actually happens, pulling the clarity out of those harsh neon washes."
