ARTS 1100 Final Project

Mundane

An experimental short film produced as a final requirement for ARTS 1100. This project breaks away from traditional narrative structures, utilizing unconventional visual techniques and atmospheric pacing to translate abstract concepts into a raw, cinematic experience.

Production Specs

  • Production Team — Neil Jacob Santiago, Alfredo Nama III, Kurt Russel Garcia, Ryan Biola, Christian Lagleva, Frauline Alvendia, Trisha Claro
  • Genre — Experimental Short Film
  • Deliverables — Conceptualization, on-location filming, and cinematic post-production.
