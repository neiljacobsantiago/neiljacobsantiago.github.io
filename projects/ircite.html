Event Coverage

IRCITE 2026: Capturing the Innovation

Lead Photographer & Team Lead • CLSU

For the International Research Conference on Information Technology Education (IRCITE), my primary objective was to cover the event with the highest possible visual fidelity. I served as the Lead Photographer, coordinating and directing a media team of four across the campus to ensure comprehensive coverage of the competition.

The Production Challenges

Armed primarily with my Nikon D780, the logistics proved to be quite demanding. The event was split between Building A and Building B, which are located far apart. We had to continuously haul gear back and forth in extremely hot weather. Furthermore, constantly transitioning between harsh outdoor sunlight and mixed indoor convention lighting required rapid, on-the-fly camera adjustments to maintain consistent quality.

Side Project: BITS Recruitment Vlog

While my core responsibility was event photography, I also sidelined as a videographer for a separate initiative. Operating independently from my IMG duties, I filmed and produced a vlog to aid the BITS recruitment process, documenting the behind-the-scenes reality of the team.
